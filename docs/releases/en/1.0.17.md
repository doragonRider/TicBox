# TicBox 1.0.17

The phone app, Local Bridge on the watch and the bundled watch module are all version 1.0.17 (versionCode 18).

## What's new

**The photo face can be edited.** Choosing a photo under Watch → Watch faces → Photo face now opens a new Edit photo page. "Edit photo and layout" opens it too, with the photo you edited since opening the page, or else the photo that's on the watch now.

- **Move, zoom and crop:** drag the photo in the round frame, pinch to zoom, and double-tap to fill the screen.
  - The selected area may reach past the photo, and that part comes out black, so a landscape or portrait photo can fit on the face whole.
  - The corners outside the circle, which the watch doesn't show, are dimmed.
- **Sent at the watch screen's resolution:** the supported watches are all 466 × 466, and the page shows how many pixels the selected area has.
  - When the area is larger, the page says it will be scaled down, with Bilinear, Bicubic or Lanczos-3 (the default).
  - When it's smaller, the page warns that it will be scaled up and may look blurry. The same three methods are offered, with Bicubic as the default.
  - Scaling down uses every pixel, so fine detail doesn't turn jagged or show moiré from skipped pixels.
- **Sharpening:** Light, Medium or Strong sharpening after scaling. Once the photo rests, the frame shows the exact picture that will be sent, so you can see what the method and sharpening do.
- **Face layout:** the photo face's own three settings can now be changed:
  - whether the time sits at the top, middle or bottom;
  - whether the date and day of the week show above the time;
  - whether the battery level shows near the bottom.

  The frame and the Photo face page draw the time, date and battery where these settings put them.
- **Sending:** photos go out as 466 × 466 PNGs. The watch saves what it receives as a quality 90 JPEG anyway, so a lossless PNG is compressed only that once. Before, photos were cropped to the middle square and sent as 480 × 480 JPEGs at quality 80.

**Reading photos:** they're turned upright the way they were taken and converted to sRGB. Very large photos are read at 4096 pixels on the long side.

## Updating

1. Open the new `ticbox-phone-1.0.17-release-<commit>.apk` on the phone to install it over the old one. Your data stays. If Play Protect says "App scan recommended", you can scan and then install, or choose "More details" → "Install without scanning". From a computer, use `adb install -r`.
2. Local Bridge and the watch module only changed their version number. There's no need to update them.

The full notes, in Chinese, are in [docs/releases/1.0.17.md](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.19/docs/releases/1.0.17.md).

## Not yet verified on a real device

- **Layout on the watch:** whether the time, date and battery land where the preview shows them after the three settings are sent. What the settings mean, and where the preview puts things, comes from the official app's own preview, not from checking a watch.
- **How the watch draws the photo:** it saves the photo again as a JPEG, then draws it straight onto the screen at the screen's width, with no bitmap in between. So a 466 × 466 photo is shown pixel for pixel. This hasn't been checked on a watch.
- **Transfer time:** a PNG is much larger than the old JPEG, usually a few hundred KB, so sending it over Bluetooth takes a few seconds longer.
- **Speed on a phone:** how smoothly dragging and zooming run on a real phone, and how quickly the final picture appears once the photo rests. This was only run in Robolectric. For a very large photo it may take about a second.
- **Editing the watch's photo:** the photo on the watch is the small preview the watch sends back, so editing it always warns about scaling up.
