# TicBox 1.0.16: the first release

This is the first published release of TicBox, an unofficial, open-source phone companion app for TicWatch watches. It can replace the official Mobvoi Health app, and it adds a toolbox for working with the watch from the phone.

TicBox is local-first. Data goes from the watch over Bluetooth to a database on your phone. The Mobvoi cloud, Google's cloud and the watch's internet proxy are off by default, and are only used after you turn them on in the app.

## What it does

Without root:

- **Notifications:** your phone's notifications on the watch, with their action buttons and text replies.
- **Calls and media:** call control and media control.
- **The watch:** watch faces, watch preferences, screen lock and watch apps.
- **Safety:** Emergency SOS, and finding the phone from the watch.
- **Health data:** heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen, steps and more, stored and charted on the phone, with day, week and month views.
- **Local Bridge features:** finding the watch from the phone, and Do Not Disturb sync. Health data also comes over through Local Bridge, a small watch app that TicBox installs for you over the watch's wireless debugging, so no computer is needed.
- **Advanced tools,** over the watch's wireless debugging: installing apps on the watch (including split APKs and data files), app management, files, clipboard, a terminal and remote screen.
- **Your data:** export and import the phone's data.

With root (Magisk 26 or later on the watch), which is optional:

- Full workouts, GPS routes and per-point data.
- A health change log, so syncs read only what changed.
- With the watch module that ships inside the app: more health monitoring settings, and notification sound and vibration per app.

See the [feature list](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.16/README.md#features) for what each one needs. What isn't implemented yet, or is more limited than in the official app, is listed under [capability gaps](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.16/docs/dev/capability-gaps.md) (in Chinese).

## What you need

- **Watch:** a TicWatch Pro 5, Pro 5 Enduro or Atlas. So far TicBox has only been tested on a TicWatch Pro 5; the Pro 5 Enduro and Atlas haven't been tried yet.
- **Wear OS:** 3.5 or 4. The Atlas ships with Wear OS 4; the Pro 5 and Pro 5 Enduro ship with 3.5 and have an official update to 4. On 3.5, the watch can't keep its data when it moves to another phone.
- **Phone:** Android 14 or later, with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

A watch can be paired with only one phone at a time. If yours is paired with Mobvoi Health now, [step 2 of the guide](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.16/README.md#step-2-put-the-watch-into-pairing-mode) explains how to move it over and keep the watch's data. A reset erases the watch, so it's the last resort.

## Getting started

1. Download `ticbox-phone-1.0.16-release-<commit>.apk` below and open it on the phone to install it. If Play Protect says "App scan recommended", you can scan and then install, or choose "More details" → "Install without scanning".
2. Follow the [getting started guide](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.16/README.md#getting-started): put the watch into pairing mode, pair, turn on notification sync, then install Local Bridge from the app for health data.

Root is optional, and nothing in the guide needs it. If you want the root features, see [root and the watch module](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.16/README.md#advanced-root-and-the-watch-module). Unlocking the watch and flashing Magisk aren't covered there: that usually erases the watch, can affect its warranty, and done wrong can leave it unable to start.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ticbox-phone-1.0.16-release-<commit>.apk` | The phone app. Local Bridge and the watch module ship inside it, so this is the only file most people need. |
| `ticbox-healthbridge-1.0.16-release-<commit>.apk` | Local Bridge on its own, for installing it on the watch by hand, for example with `adb install`. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `manifest.json`, `README.txt` | Checksums of both APKs, the build's details, and the signing certificate's SHA-256. |

## Changes since the previous builds

If you built TicBox from source before, these are the changes since 1.0.15:

- **A new app icon:** a flat round watch in three colors to choose from under Settings → Appearance → App icon. The colors are **Warm white** (the default), **Navy** and **Mist**. All three share a new themed icon, and Local Bridge on the watch uses Warm white.
  - **If you chose Slate or White dial in 1.0.15, switch back to the default icon in 1.0.15 before you update.** The launcher entries behind them were renamed, so TicBox would otherwise disappear from your launcher.
  - If it already has: open TicBox with the "Current watch face" shortcut on your home screen, if you added it, and tap Warm white under Settings → Appearance → App icon. Without that shortcut, the only way back is from a computer: `adb shell pm enable dev.ticbox.companion/.MainActivity`.
- **A Month view on health detail pages,** after Day and Week: the month that ends on the selected date, with a bar for each day.
- **Detail pages update in place when you change the date:** each part changes when the new date's data is ready, instead of the whole page reloading. The 7 days before and the day after are read ahead, so Previous day, Next day and Week usually don't have to wait.
- **The hourly steps chart:** the bars now touch, with no gaps, and very small values no longer reach below the axis.

The full notes, in Chinese, are in [docs/releases/1.0.16.md](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.16/docs/releases/1.0.16.md).
