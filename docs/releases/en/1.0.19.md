# TicBox 1.0.19

The phone app, Local Bridge on the watch and the bundled watch module are all version 1.0.19 (versionCode 20).

These are the changes since 1.0.16, the last release. Versions 1.0.17 and 1.0.18 weren't released on their own, and everything they brought is here too. Everything in this release is about the photo face: the watch face that shows a photo of your choice behind the time.

## Edit the photo before it goes to the watch

Choosing a photo under Watch → Watch faces → Photo face now opens an Edit photo page. "Edit photo and layout" opens it too, with the photo you last sent from TicBox.

- **Move, zoom and crop:** drag the photo in the round frame, pinch to zoom, and double-tap to fill the screen.
  - The selected area may reach past the photo, and that part comes out black, so a landscape or portrait photo can fit on the face whole.
  - The corners outside the circle, which the watch doesn't show, are dimmed.
- **Sent at the watch screen's resolution:** the supported watches are all 466 × 466, and the page shows how many pixels the selected area has.
  - When the area is larger, the page says it will be scaled down, with Bilinear, Bicubic or Lanczos-3 (the default). Every pixel is used, so fine detail doesn't turn jagged or show moiré.
  - When it's smaller, the page warns that it will be scaled up and may look blurry, with the same three methods and Bicubic as the default.
- **Sharpening:** Light, Medium or Strong sharpening after scaling. Once the photo rests, the frame shows exactly the picture that will be sent.
- **Face layout:** the photo face's own settings can now be changed:
  - whether the time sits at the top, middle or bottom;
  - whether the date and day of the week show above the time;
  - whether the battery level shows near the bottom.

  The frame and the Photo face page show where they go.
- **Better quality:** photos go out as 466 × 466 PNGs. The watch saves what it receives as a JPEG anyway, so the photo is compressed only once. Before, photos were cropped to the middle square and sent as 480 × 480 JPEGs at quality 80.
- **Photos read correctly:** they're turned upright the way they were taken and converted to sRGB. Very large photos are read at 4096 pixels on the long side.

## The watch switches to the photo face

- Before, sending only saved the photo on the watch. The photo face (called Image on the watch) isn't one of its favorites by default, so nothing changed on the watch, and "Use this face" didn't switch either.
- Now, after a photo is sent and when you tap "Use this face", TicBox adds the Image face to the favorites if needed and makes it the current face. If the face can't be found, or the watch won't add it, TicBox tells you to add it under Watch faces → More faces yourself.
- **Sending shows its progress:** a bar below the button goes through preparing the photo, sending it to the watch and switching the watch's face. The page scrolls to it, and the editor closes once the watch shows the photo.
  - If you go back early, the Photo face page keeps showing the progress.
  - Leaving the Photo face page doesn't stop the send. The result shows in a message at the bottom of the screen.

## Your photo, wherever the face is shown

The watch's own preview of the photo face is a stock picture. TicBox now draws the photo on the watch instead, with the time, date and battery as you set them:

- on the watch on the home page, and in the "Current watch face" shortcut, when the photo face is the current face;
- on the photo face's card under Watch → Watch faces.

## Editing again starts from your original

The watch can only hand back a 300 pixel wide copy of its photo. Once the watch accepts a photo, TicBox now keeps the original on the phone, with its framing, scaling method and sharpening, so editing again starts where you left off at full quality.

- The original is kept in the app's private storage, outside system backups.
- Only the last photo sent is kept, and photos over 64 MB aren't kept.
- If you haven't sent a photo from TicBox yet, editing starts from the watch's small copy, and the page warns that it's smaller than the screen.

## Updating

1. Open the new `ticbox-phone-1.0.19-release-<commit>.apk` on the phone to install it over the old one. Your data stays. If Play Protect says "App scan recommended", you can scan and then install, or choose "More details" → "Install without scanning". From a computer, use `adb install -r`.
2. Local Bridge and the watch module only changed their version number since 1.0.16. There's no need to update them.

## Notes

- The progress moves in steps. How long sending takes depends on Bluetooth, and the bar doesn't move while the photo is on its way. A PNG is larger than the old JPEG, usually a few hundred KB, so sending takes a few seconds longer.
- TicBox recognizes the photo face once the Photo face page has been opened, or once TicBox has switched to it. Until then, the home page and the Watch faces page show the watch's stock picture.
- The time, date and battery are drawn where the official app's preview puts them. Whether the watch places them exactly the same way hasn't been measured on a watch.
