# TicBox 1.0.18

The phone app, Local Bridge on the watch and the bundled watch module are all version 1.0.18 (versionCode 19).

## Fixes

- **The watch now switches to the photo face after a photo is sent.**
  - Before, the photo was only saved on the watch. The photo face (called Image on the watch) isn't one of its favorites by default, so nothing changed on the watch, and "Use this face" didn't switch either.
  - Now, after "Send to watch" succeeds and when you tap "Use this face", TicBox adds the Image face to the favorites and makes it the current face, as adding it under Watch faces → More faces and choosing it would.
  - Nothing is switched if it's already the current face. If the face can't be found, or the watch won't add it, TicBox tells you to add it under More faces yourself.
- **Editing again no longer gives a 300 × 300 photo.**
  - The watch only hands back a 300 pixel wide preview, and editing again after leaving the Photo face page started from that.
  - Now, once the watch accepts a photo, TicBox keeps the original on the phone, with its framing, scaling method and sharpening, and editing again starts from there.
  - The original is kept in the app's private storage, outside system backups. Only the last photo sent is kept, and photos over 64 MB aren't kept.
  - If you've never sent a photo from TicBox, editing still starts from the watch's preview, and the page warns that it's smaller than the screen.
- **The home page shows your photo.** When the photo face is the current face, the watch on the home page and the "Current watch face" shortcut used to show the Image face's stock picture. They now show the photo on the watch, with the time, date and battery as you set them.

## Updating

1. Open the new `ticbox-phone-1.0.18-release-<commit>.apk` on the phone to install it over the old one. Your data stays. If Play Protect says "App scan recommended", you can scan and then install, or choose "More details" → "Install without scanning". From a computer, use `adb install -r`.
2. Local Bridge and the watch module only changed their version number. There's no need to update them.

The full notes, in Chinese, are in [docs/releases/1.0.18.md](https://github.com/doragonRider/TicBox/blob/v1.0.18/docs/releases/1.0.18.md).

## Not yet verified on a real device

- **Switching on a real watch:** adding the Image face and switching to it uses TicBox's existing favorites protocol. Whether the class name in the photo face's settings matches the watch's face catalog was only tried on the demo watch.
- **Recognizing the photo face on the home page:** TicBox only knows which face it is after the Photo face page has been opened once, or after it has switched to it once.
- **The framing when editing again:** the same original reads at the same size every time, so the framing should come back as it was.
